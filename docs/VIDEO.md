**Title**

Gün: rotating savings circles (altın günü) on gno.land

**Description**

Gün puts a rotating savings circle (the Turkish "altın günü", also known as a tanda, susu, hui or chit fund) in a gno.land realm. The coins sit with the code, the books are public, and the rules are enforced instead of remembered.

In this demo, recorded on the Onyx testnet:
0:00 What a savings circle is, and why it runs on trust
0:16 Creating a circle and joining it by locking collateral
0:38 Round 1: payments arrive, the pot goes to Ayşe
1:06 Round 2: Zeynep does not pay; anyone settles the overdue round and her collateral covers it
1:35 The circle completes; members withdraw what they are owed
1:55 Open books, what a circle guarantees, and the rules package
